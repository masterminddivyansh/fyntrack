About FynTrack

Clear information for a trustworthy finance tools platform.

What is FynTrack?

FynTrack is a finance tools platform designed to make everyday money decisions easier to understand. We build calculators for savings, investments, loans, taxes and practical planning.

Our approach

We focus on transparent inputs, understandable formulas, useful assumptions and clean interfaces. Public calculators are designed to work without an account.

What we are building

FynTrack is starting with calculators and will gradually expand into tracking and planning tools while keeping the experience simple and useful.
